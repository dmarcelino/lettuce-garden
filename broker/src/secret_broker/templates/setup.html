Create your vault

Your vault keeps secrets such as addresses, cards, ID documents and passwords. Your agents can ask to use them, and you decide.

Everything in it is encrypted with a passphrase that only you know. Choose a long one you will remember, such as four or five unrelated words.

Nobody can recover a forgotten passphrase, not even whoever runs this server. If you forget it, the only way back is to erase the vault and start again.
